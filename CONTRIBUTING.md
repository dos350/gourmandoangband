## Contributing

the scope of this variant is an enhanced classic vanilla Angband; we aint goin super wild;
we aint changing any fundamentals or adding anything outside the classic town/dungeon

if u want to contribute to gourmandoAngband, go right ahead and submit a patch or watever;
but yeah; its mainly my own personal thing, so if u want to just do something crazy, go fork angband urself instead;
thanks
